At Vasak we believe it is important to keep the community informed about the changes made to the project, so we created a news channel on **Telegram** where the most important news about the project will be published. It is completely public and anyone can join.

To join the channel, you can do it from the following link: [https://t.me/VasakOSNews](https://t.me/VasakOSNews)

## Content

The most important news about the project will be published on the channel, for example:

- New VasakOS releases
- New releases of the VasakOS applications
- New releases of the VasakOS packages
- New articles on the blog
- New videos on the YouTube channel
- Information about libraries and tools
- Community events
- Information about related projects
- And much more!

If you have any question or suggestion, you can reach us on the community Telegram channel: [https://t.me/VasakOS](https://t.me/VasakOS)

Greetings!
