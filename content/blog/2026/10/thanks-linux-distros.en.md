From Italy, **[Linux Distros](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h5K1UfeYFkw)** devoted a video to VasakOS with an angle we especially liked: looking at **how it's built**. Not the umpteenth wallpaper, but the fact that the desktop, the main applications and the services are made on purpose for the system —with Rust and CSS on top of Tauri and Vue, an Arch base, Wayland with Wayfire, systemd and pacman—. **Grazie mille** for the technical spotlight.

## What you talked about

This video was, above all, a **presentation of the architecture**: what VasakOS is and why the way it's built sets it apart. You didn't flag any concrete problems to fix, so there's no to-do list to hand you here —and we appreciate it, because understanding the idea is half the battle—.

What we do take away is the push to keep going where we work the most: WebKit **performance** and the overall polish of the desktop, which is a continuous improvement. If something bugs you on a future test, we'll gladly add it to the list.

## Onward

Having the project cross the language barrier and reach the Italian community makes us very happy. **Thanks again** — the door's open for whatever you'd like to try.
