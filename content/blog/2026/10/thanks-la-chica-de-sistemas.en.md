In her **Distrofest 2026**, the yearly distro ranking by **[La Chica de Sistemas](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9eLXdJIy7GE)**, VasakOS earned an **honorable mention**. For such a young project, showing up there —among distros with years behind them— means a lot. **Thank you, Alexia**, for the love and for bringing it to the festival.

## What she said

Her mention highlighted two things that are, precisely, the heart of the project: that it follows a **minimalist line**, without preloading the distro with a thousand things nobody asked for, and that there's **a serious design and architecture decision** behind it. What sets it apart, she said, is that it comes with its **own desktop environment**, Vasak Desktop. In her words, "a project that's really well put together".

Hearing that from someone who makes a living administering systems confirms we're on the right track.

## Onward

VasakOS isn't available to the general public yet, but you can follow its development at [os.vasak.net.ar](https://os.vasak.net.ar/). **Thanks again for the mention** — it made our day.
