**[LinuxLinks](https://www.linuxlinks.com/vasakos-arch-linux-based-distribution/)** published an article about VasakOS, an Arch-based distribution with its own desktop, Wayland and native applications written in Rust.

We're glad they stopped at what makes VasakOS different: a system built from the ground up on Arch, with its own desktop on Wayland and applications written in Rust and Tauri, without relying on themed foreign environments.

**Thank you** for the article and for giving visibility to the project.

## What you talked about

The article highlights that VasakOS bets on its own desktop on Wayland, native Rust apps and an interface that can be restyled with CSS without recompiling. That's exactly what we're building: a coherent system, made on purpose for VasakOS, and not just a repackaged environment.

If you try VasakOS and something bugs you, let us know and we'll gladly add it to the improvements list.

## Onward

Having the project reach more international communities makes us very happy. **Thanks again** — the door is open for whatever you'd like to try or comment.
