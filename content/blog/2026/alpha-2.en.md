We tried to start 2026 with everything, so we decided to release alpha 2 and to make many changes to the way we have been working...

## What will I find in Alpha 2?

We already have a configured icon pack, a system that has many more options, and some interesting news you can find in the [official changelog](/en/changelogs/20260106/).

## Can I install Alpha 2 on my PC?

Not only do we not recommend installing this version on a PC, given the *possible instability* and the *lack of features*, but to avoid complications we did not include an installer in the ISO that you can get in the downloads area. For now we are aiming to release an ISO so that the experience can be reviewed; in future ISO releases, however, we will be adding the installer so that a virtual machine can be made persistent.

## Where do I report bugs or share my experiences?

This is extremely important to us, so you have [the Telegram group](https://t.me/VasakOS) at your disposal to share your experiences or report bugs; it can also be done from the repositories themselves.
