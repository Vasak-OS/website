{{/* A date in the language of the site. This used to look the month and weekday up in data/meses.yml and data/dias.yml, which held the Spanish names, and only when the language was Spanish. Two problems: adding a language meant editing a lookup table instead of writing the month down once, and every language that was not Spanish fell through to Go's default format, which is always American English — "Friday, March 3, 2006" on a page written in Spanish. `time.Format` and `.Format` both take the layout from a fixed table of reference components and resolve the *names* against the site's language, so there is nothing left to translate here. Hugo localises them for es and en out of the box. The `de` between day, month and year is not a word that translates: English does not use it, and Spanish capitalises neither the weekday nor the month in running text, so no post-processing is needed either. */}} {{- $skeleton := "Monday, January 2, 2006" -}} {{- if eq site.Language.Lang "es" -}} {{- $skeleton = "lunes, 2 de January de 2006" -}} {{- end -}} {{- return time.Format $skeleton . -}}
